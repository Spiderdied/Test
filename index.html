Articles — Definitions
Complete reference from the textbook
What is an Article?

Definition

An article is an adjective that modifies nouns. English has two articles: a/an (the indefinite article) and the (the definite article). A/an modifies non-specific nouns; the modifies specific or particular nouns.

I. The Indefinite Article

A / An — General Rule

The indefinite article refers to no specific person or thing. A is used before a consonant sound or a vowel sounded like a consonant; an is used before a vowel sound or a mute h. It is the same for all genders.

a man · a university · an apple · an hour · an honest man
Indefinite Article — Use 1

First mention of a singular countable noun

Used before a singular countable noun mentioned for the first time, representing no particular person or thing.

I see a man. · I need a pen. · A house has a roof.
Indefinite Article — Use 2

A noun as an example of a class

Used before a singular countable noun representing a whole class of things.

A child needs love. · A banana is yellow. · A cow has horns.
Indefinite Article — Use 3

Names of jobs or professions

The indefinite article is used before names of occupations and professions.

I am a teacher. · He is a farmer. · She is a doctor.
Indefinite Article — Use 4

Expressions of quantity and certain numbers

Used in fixed quantity expressions and before specific numbers.

a lot of · a great deal of · a couple · half a dozen · a hundred · a thousand · a million · a score (20) · a gross (144)
Indefinite Article — Use 5

Exclamations before singular countable nouns

What a nice day! · What a smart answer!
Indefinite Article — Use 6

Before Mr. / Mrs. / Miss + surname

The indefinite article may be placed before a title and surname to indicate an unfamiliar person.

a Mr. Aly · a Mrs. Johns
Omission of A / An

When NOT to use a / an

  • Before uncountable nouns
  • Before plural nouns
  • Before names of meals, except when preceded by an adjective
  • In double expressions, particularly with prepositions (arm in arm · day after day · from top to bottom · husband and wife · inch by inch · on land and sea · with knife and fork)
  • Names of illnesses and pains are usually uncountable with no article in standard British English — exceptions: a cold · a headache · a sore throat

N.B. When the name of a meal is preceded by an adjective, the indefinite article is used before the adjective. When the meal is specific or special, it is preceded by the definite article.

We had a very delicious dinner. · The dinner they served last evening was very delicious.
II. The Definite Article

The — General Rule

The definite article refers to a specific person, place, or thing. It is the same for singular and plural and for all genders.

the man / the men · the woman / the women · the mobile / the mobiles
Definite Article — Use 1

Unique or one-of-a-kind nouns

Used before nouns of which there is only one, or which are considered unique.

the sun · the moon · the earth · the sky
Definite Article — Use 2

Second mention of a noun

Used before a noun that has become definite as a result of being mentioned a second time.

His car hit a tree. You can still see the mark on the tree.
Definite Article — Use 3

Noun made definite by a phrase or clause

The young man in blue. · This is the hotel where I met him.
Definite Article — Use 4

Noun representing one particular thing by locality

Ahmed is in the garden. · Please pass the salt.
Definite Article — Use 5

Superlatives and ordinal numbers

He is the most intelligent student I have ever met. · I live on the second floor.
Definite Article — Use 6

Singular noun representing a class of objects

The fox is a very cunning animal. · The red rose is my favourite flower.
Definite Article — Use 7

Adjective representing a class of people

the poor · the rich · the old · the young
Definite Article — Use 8

Nationality words

The Egyptians · The Palestinians · The Chinese
Definite Article — Use 9

Names of musical instruments

the drum · the flute · the guitar · the piano
Definite Article — Use 10

Rivers, seas, oceans, mountain chains, and some countries

The Nile · The Red Sea · The Mediterranean · The Atlantic Ocean · The Alps · The Sudan
Definite Article — Use 11

Plural surnames

The Olsons
III. Zero Article

No Article (Ø)

No article is used with nouns like school and hospital when referring to their normal purpose. Other such nouns include: bed, church, class, college, prison, sea, town, university, work. When not referring to the purpose, the or a/an is used.

Aly has gone to school (to learn). · He is at school. · Zaid is in hospital (ill). — vs — Aly has gone to the school for a meeting.
Useful Abbreviations

i.e. and N.B.

  • i.e. — abbreviation for Latin id est; that is to say
  • N.B., n b — nota bene; note well
